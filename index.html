AlgoScope · Setup Guide
How to Export Your Trade CSV
Two export formats supported: the Trade List (individual trades, recommended) and the Performance Report (summary stats). AlgoScope auto-detects which one you upload.
1
Export the Trade List (Recommended)
1
Open NinjaTrader 8 and connect to your account or simulation account
2
From the top menu go to New → Account Performance
3
Select your account and set your desired date range at the top
4
Click the Trades tab (not the Summary tab)
5
Right-click anywhere in the trade table → select Export to CSV
6
Save the file and upload it here — AlgoScope will auto-detect the format
2
Alternative: Performance Report Export
1
In Account Performance, click the Performance tab
2
Click the Export button (top right of the panel) → Export to CSV
3
This exports a summary report — AlgoScope will parse this format too, though the Trade List gives richer per-trade analytics
Pro tip: Export one strategy at a time and give each a clear name when importing (e.g. "ORB ES v2" or "ADX Breakout NQ"). This makes comparison much cleaner in AlgoScope.
Tradovate CSV exports include your completed order history with P&L per trade. AlgoScope auto-detects the Tradovate format on upload.
1
Log in to your account at tradovate.com or the Tradovate desktop app
2
Click your Account icon (top right) → select Account Details
3
Navigate to the History tab, then select the Orders sub-tab
4
Set your date range using the date picker at the top of the table
5
Click the Export button (download icon, top right of the table) and save as CSV
6
Upload the CSV to AlgoScope — name it after the strategy you ran during that period
Note: If you run multiple strategies in Tradovate on the same account, filter by instrument or date range per strategy before exporting, so each CSV represents one strategy cleanly.
✅  One strategy per CSV
AlgoScope compares strategies against each other. Upload a separate CSV for each algo or time period you want to compare. You can upload as many as you like.
✅  Give strategies clear names
When importing, name each strategy descriptively — "ORB ES Live 2024", "ADX NQ Sim Q1" etc. These names appear across all charts and tables.
⚠️  "No trades found" after uploading?
Make sure you're exporting the Trades tab (not the Orders or Positions tab). AlgoScope expects completed round-trip trades with P&L per row. If using NinjaTrader, try both the Trade List and Performance Report exports.
✅  Your data never leaves your browser
AlgoScope runs 100% locally. No server, no account, no data sent anywhere. You can even use it offline once the page has loaded. Your trade data stays on your machine.
✅  Comparing sim vs live?
Export your sim account trades as one CSV and live account trades as another. Import both with names like "ORB ES Sim" and "ORB ES Live" to see how your live performance compares to simulation.
⚠️  Dates look wrong or missing?
Make sure your NinjaTrader date format is set to MM/DD/YYYY in your Windows regional settings. AlgoScope parses the standard NinjaTrader date format. Tradovate exports are ISO format and parse automatically.
